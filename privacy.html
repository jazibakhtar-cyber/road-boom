Road Boom Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how Road Boom handles information.

Current data handling

Road Boom does not require an account. The game may store gameplay settings and progress locally on your device.

Advertising

Road Boom may add Google AdMob advertising in a future update. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, advertising data, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, and interaction data as described in Google's policies and subject to applicable consent requirements.

Children and consent

Advertising and consent settings will be configured for the game's intended audience and applicable regional requirements before ads are enabled.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards for information handled by the game. Third-party services determine their own retention periods under their policies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Road Boom's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
